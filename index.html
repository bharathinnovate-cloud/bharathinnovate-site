Who we are

Bharath Innovate is an Indian FMCG company engaged in the manufacturing, marketing and distribution of high quality food products. We keep the highest standards of hygiene and food safety at every step.

Our flagship brand, South Harvest, is inspired by the culinary heritage of South India. With a strong focus on quality, innovation and customer satisfaction, we aim to bring trusted, flavourful and wholesome products to households and businesses across India.

Alongside South Harvest, we distribute a variety of food and FMCG brands in our territory, working as the local partner for manufacturers.
